How to play

  • Turn a layer – drag any sticker the way you want it to move. Release to snap; a quick flick turns it too.
  • Spin the cube – drag the dark background in any direction, as many full turns as you like. Flick for momentum, scroll or pinch to zoom, double-tap to reset the view.
  • Keyboard – U D L R F B turn faces (hold Shift to reverse). M E S turn middle slices on odd sizes. Arrow keys spin the view.
  • Space scramble · Ctrl+Z undo · Ctrl+Y redo · P auto-spin · Home reset view
  • Algorithms – open Moves, type a sequence like R U R' U' (or pick a preset) and press Run.
  • Timer starts on your first move after a scramble and stops when you solve it. Solve replays your history backwards.
  • Your cube is saved automatically, so you can close the page and carry on later.
