How to play

  • Turn a layer – drag any sticker along the direction you want it to move. Release to snap; a quick flick turns it too.
  • Look around – drag the dark background (or use the arrow keys). Scroll or pinch to zoom.
  • Keyboard – U D L R F B turn faces, hold Shift for the reverse. M E S turn the middle slices.
  • Space scramble · Ctrl+Z undo · Ctrl+Y redo
  • Timer starts on your first move after a scramble and stops when the cube is solved.
  • Solve replays your move history backwards to restore the cube.
